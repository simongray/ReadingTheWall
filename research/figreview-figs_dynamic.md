The 5th frame shows the catch as a ghost. Six frames would allow only 92 units each, too narrow for an honest straight-arm sink: it forced sitting poses. So "set the feet, look up" is merged into the sink frame. Inserted before the Dyno entry.

Library notes:
- `_solve_leg` flips the foot normal toward the hip with a sign test. When the hip lies almost on the foot's axis (a pointed foot hanging under the leg), two nearly identical feet can solve to different ankles. The fix was to change the foot angle by 10–15°.
- `Climber.warn()` checks ankles only in side views.
