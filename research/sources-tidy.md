# Sources tidy (2026-10-31)

Changes to `research/*-sources.yml`, one line per changed field. Keys, URLs
and notes are unchanged. `book/src-map.typ` was regenerated with
`tools/merge_sources.py`.

Rules applied:

- Titles of web pages, forum threads and reports use headline-style title
  case: articles, conjunctions and prepositions in lower case unless they
  open the title or a subtitle. Journal and conference titles keep the case
  they were published in; German titles keep German capitalisation.
- Titles use typographic apostrophes (’) and double quotes (“ ”), as the
  body text does, and " – " between a title and its series.
- An author string with a lower-case word (“Lacrux editors”, “Mountain
  Project forum”) is read by Hayagriva as a name particle and printed
  partly or not at all. Organisations with lower-case words in their name
  are now written as `{name: "…"}`.
- Pages without a byline name the site as author, in one form per site:
  Climbing.com, Movement Climbing, Wikipedia, Lacrux, Mountain Project
  forum.
- Report-type entries print no URL in this style; online-only documents
  (B1, B2, C42, G56) are now `web`. G10 and G12 stay reports.

Left as they were:

- E17 “Training to ... Part 3”: the page is paywalled (HTTP 402) and the
  full title could not be found.
- Duplicates the merge script can't see, because their URLs differ: A19/B56,
  A21/C34, A22/B58, B30/C4, E58/F24, B4/C44, A43/F35 and A44/F44 (different
  forum message IDs). Their fields now match, so they merge once the URLs
  are made to agree. E34/G48 now share a DOI and are merged.
- “Bruijn, B. de”, “Middelkoop, M. van” and “Valle Jou, M. del” are how
  the Springer style places name particles.
