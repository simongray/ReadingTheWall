# Photo check 3: climbers' poses

This pass follows up the doubts that the first photo check left in FOLLOW-UPS.md, then looks at full-body figures whose pose looked doubtful on sight. It changes no figure. The photos were reference only and stay out of the repository. Video stills come from YouTube's preview storyboards (small frames of the whole video), with the times given. Sides are the climber's own: in a back view the climber's left is on the reader's left.

## Out of scope or not found

- Pivoting is a shoe seen from above, so it falls under the views from above.
- Tic-tac: still no climbing photo or video. The only stills are from parkour, as in the first check.
- Hip pump, Counter-kick, Run-and-jump and Latching (c) weren't searched again in this pass. On sight none of them looked wrong.
- Other full-body figures looked at on sight (sidepull, undercling, gaston, palming, mantle, volume mantle, toe pull, precise footwork, hip pop, lunge, double clutch, jump start) showed nothing doubtful enough to check.

## Ranked fixes

1. **Knee bar:** put the free foot on the wall. The drawing contradicts its own how-to step and most photos.
2. **High step (b) and Rockover (1, 2):** turn the high foot out about 45° toward the knee. It's the most visible pose error on the V1 pages, and it repeats across two figures.
3. **Traverse:** turn the right hip in instead of the left, so the back flag holds up in 3D and the drawing order no longer has to fake it.
4. **Hand–foot match (b):** turn the high foot out, as for the high step. It's smaller on the page, but the fix is the same.
5. **Heel–toe cam:** optionally tilt the shin out. It's a minor cosmetic change.
6. **Stemming:** no figure change. The text could add "up" for a palm at shoulder height.
