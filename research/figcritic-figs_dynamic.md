# Figure critic: figs_dynamic (chapter 06, dynamic movement)

All figures and filmstrip frames were checked at 140–300 dpi. Joint angles were measured for every drawn climber, ghosts included. Side views were also checked for knees pushed through the wall line. The build prints no warnings for this module. `latching` was left alone, because another agent is redrawing it.

- **filmstrip-dyno**: in frame 2 the knee was 3 cm inside the wall and in frame 3 18 cm inside: with the feet on the wall, a bent knee bends into it. The foothold moved down to W(70), 100 cm below the start holds. Frame 2 is now the arched drive the text describes: hips in, shoulders back, arms nearly straight, legs at 126°. In frame 3 the legs are straight (159°) and the toes are just off the hold and pointed. Frame 1 sinks a little less (hip flexion 148°). In frame 3 the head tips back so it shows beside the raised arms.
- **dyno**: (b) had the same knee in the wall and now uses the frame-2 pose; (a) uses the new sink. The "let go late" leader crossed the body and now comes from above. The block arrow no longer lies on the legs.
- **hip-pump**: (a) the left knee pointed against its toes; it now bends out. The dip arrow now passes under the crotch, not over the thigh. The start ghost's right foot did not reach its hold; the hips moved slightly.
- **lunge**: (b) the throwing-side foot pointed against the dropped knee; it now points down.
- **counter-kick**: (b) the kicking leg crosses behind the standing leg by design (`allow_cross`). It is now drawn in front, nearer the viewer, as in the back flag.
- **hip-pop**: the backstep knee pointed up while the toes pointed the other way, and a near-horizontal leg can't show an inward knee. Now drawn as a drop knee (foot down, knee in below it), which the summary allows. Label "drop knee", panel caption "sit back on the drop knee". Chapter caption edited: "(a) Set a drop knee and let your hips sag." The block arrow moved off the legs.
- **pogo**: (a) the back-swung leg crosses by design (`allow_cross`). Its knee now bends up, the way the toes point, and the leg is straighter. The "left foot pushes" label moved off the force arrow.
- **lache**: both hanging feet pointed against their knees; foot angles flipped. The "no footholds" label moved off the ghost's shoe.
- **tic-tac**: the push leg was folded to 41°. The hips start higher, so the knee is 74°. The block arrow crossed the flying climber's legs and was removed; the force arrow shows the push direction.
- **double-clutch**: the start ghost was folded to 47–49° (unreported, because only the solid climber was checked). The start footholds are now lower and wider, so the knees are 65–71°. The block arrow now passes between the legs.
- **paddle-dyno**: the block arrow ended on a knee; it now stops short.
- **foot-catch**: the force arrow lay on the shin; it now sits above the toe.
- **jump-start**: the "arms bent" label collided with the panel letter.
- **deadpoint, double-dyno, cutting-loose, run-and-jump, campus-move**: checked, no change.

Still doubtful: in filmstrip-dyno frame 3 the raised arms still cover part of the head. That is true to a profile view, but a reader may read it as a missing head.
