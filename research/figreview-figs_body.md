# Figure review: figs_body (chapter 04, body positioning)

All 18 figures reviewed against `ILLUSTRATION.md` and the entries in `04-body.typ`. The final build prints no warnings. `Climber.warn()` does not check knee or elbow angles in back views, so every pose was also checked for interior knee and elbow angles. Many figures had knees folded to 30–45° because the hands sat only 120–140 cm above the feet. Holds are now spaced about 170–190 cm apart for standing positions.

New helpers: `_plan(..., spin=)` adds a turn arrow to the hips-from-above inset. `_door()` is a top-down inset of a barn door swinging open. `_arm()` draws an arm through an explicit elbow, for a forearm pointing into the wall. `_back_flagger()` is shared with the barn-door concept figure. Hip turn and backstep share one problem (`SIDE_*` holds) and one pose (`_turned`).

- **frog**: (a) knees at 40° (full squat), so the feet are now lower and the hands higher, with knees at 60–65°. (b) Side view: thighs foreshortened (knees point to the sides) with a (thigh, shin) pair.
- **hip-turn**: (b) right arm bent (87°) and target barely above the holding hand. Rebuilt so the climber leans off a straight right arm (about 155°) with the left foot on its outside edge under the sidepull. The backstepped knee now points the way the toes point (it pointed left while the toe pointed right, which a leg can't do). The inset now has a turn arrow. Doubtful: in a back view the turn narrows the shoulders, so the reach gain shows only as higher hips.
- **backstep**: left arm hung uselessly in (b), and the rotation arrow was ambiguous. (a) uses the hip-turn pose, with the plumb line by the heel. (b) has the foot behind the line, hips turning out (inset with arrow) and the hand short of the hold. Panel captions now say "hips stay in" and "hips spin out".
- **drop-knee**: (a) right knee folded to 28° and left elbow at 50°. Rebuilt: (a) square on with the high foot on its inside edge and the knee up. (b) knee dropped below the foot, foot on its outside edge, thigh foreshortened, opposite force arrows clear of the shoes, inset with turn arrow.
- **flag**: (a) the body turned the wrong way for a door opening (right hip in), and the dangling leg crossed the standing leg. (b) the flag leg was bent. Now (a) shows the CoG right of the hinge and a top-down door inset. (b) has the leg stretched out with the foot on the wall and the hips over the standing foot, which points into the wall.
- **back-flag**: standing knee folded and the crossing leg kinked at the knee. Now the hips sit over the left foot, so the CoG is on the hinge, and the right leg runs straight across behind the left calf.
- **inside-flag**: (a) showed two footholds; the text says one. (b) the free leg was sharply bent and the hips not turned. Now (a) has one low foothold plus a smear. (b) has the right hip in, the right leg hidden behind the left and emerging on the wall side, near-straight limbs and an inset.
- **twist-lock**: "elbow to ribs" pointed at an elbow flared out at shoulder height, and (a) was a deep squat. Rebuilt: (a) arm straight, hips half turned, shoulders square. (b) body pushed up, hips and shoulders turned, a custom arm with the elbow down by the ribs, feet squeezing (arrows) and a target overhead. Follows the chapter text: the reaching-side (left) shoulder turns in.
- **lock-off**: knees jammed into the chest and through the wall. Now the feet are lower and the knees turned out (foreshortened thighs), with the hand locked beside the shoulder.
- **layback**: a cramped ball with the arms hidden. Now straight arms, the body leaning back, staggered feet and opposite force arrows (hands pull out, feet push in).
- **stemming**: the palm's fingers pointed up and the heels were high. Now the palm presses with the fingers down, heels are low (toes up) and the arrows sit at the contacts.
- **cross-through**: a 110 cm hand move and the left arm folded across the face. Now a 75 cm cross, the left elbow down, clear labels.
- **matching**: unchanged (close-up, fine).
- **compression**: crucifix pose with straight horizontal arms. Now one big volume whose side faces face away from each other, palms pressing inward with the elbows down, and four inward arrows. Caption edited.
- **body-tension**: (b) arms bent. Now straight arms with the hips held up, a tighter crop, and an unclipped head and labels.
- **pre-loading**: (b) "arms straight" was 133°. Now 169°, with the hip-drive block arrow moved off the legs.
- **opposition**: deep squat and the arrows far from the hands. Fixed.
- **bumping**: left elbow folded to 30°. Holds respaced; the start, intermediate and target are labelled with steps 1 and 2.

Still doubtful: the drop-knee and back-flag shoe drawings (back views can only show feet in profile or heel-on). Also the twist-lock forearm, which is drawn very short because it points into the wall.
